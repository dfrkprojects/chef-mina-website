Tips for Best Results:

  • Colors: Use greens and earth tones. The palettes above work together!
  • Width: Keep ornaments 140-180px for balance with large titles.
  • Mix & Match: Use different ornaments on left and right for visual interest.
  • Opacity: Add opacity="0.7" to ornament SVG for subtle, elegant effect.
  • Scaling: Adjust viewBox to make designs wider or taller as needed.
